# Slice 4: Analyst deep dive with coordinated views. Spec

Depends on slices 0 to 3. Inputs: design-system.md §4 (density), §6.4, §6.10; domain-model.md §5, §13; user-flows.md slice 4; architecture.md §8.

## 1. Outcome
In Analyst mode the dashboard shows the basis selector, the market grid, the time series, the radar and the heat map, all driven by Context, each with View as table. The grid shows the markets of the current entity (portfolio → all 13; region → its markets; market → that market's products as rows).

## 2. View models (`src/features/analyst/viewModel.ts`, server-safe)
- `gridVM(ds, ctx)`: rows for `marketsIn(entity)` or, when a market is selected, one row per product with columns Product, Revenue, Attainment, Growth, Units, Trend. Market rows: Market (name, iso2), Status, Revenue, Attainment, Growth, Share, Trend (24 actuals in k$), AskWhy label. Sorted by revenue, largest first, for market and product rows; the Revenue header shows the sort (arrow, `aria-sort="descending"`) and the caption says "Sorted by revenue". Row values as preview-spec.md §9.2 (order is by revenue, not the preview's order). Market rows follow the selected period (month basis; basis applies to the tiles and map only): at the latest month the numbers are the `kpis` values (Source), at any other period they are derived from the series and the caption says Derived. Status and share are given only for the latest month and are shown as given.
- `timeSeriesVM(ds, ctx)`: 24 points `{period, actual, forecast, priorYear, attainment}` for the entity; anomalies for the market (only when entity is market or product of that market); title `${entityName} · Revenue vs forecast vs prior year`; `<desc>` text generated: "Line chart of {entity} monthly revenue from Jun 2024 to May 2026. Actual {above|below} forecast in {n} of 24 months{; anomaly: … if any}. Table available via View as table."
- `radarVM(ds, ctx)`: axes `["Attainment", "Growth", "Share (given)"]`; one series per market in the current region (or the 4 regions when at portfolio, using derived attainment and growth and **no** share axis: then the axes are Attainment, Growth, Revenue share of portfolio computed as region revenue / portfolio revenue, labelled "Revenue share (derived)"); values normalised per axis to 0..1 by min–max across the shown series, raw values kept for the tooltip and table. Colours `--cat-1..6` in the fixed order of markets by name; never more than 6 series (Europe has 5, APAC 4).
- `heatMapVM(ds, ctx)`: rows = markets of the entity (or the 13 at portfolio), columns = 24 periods, cell = attainment % (1 decimal) with colour from `attainmentDiverging`; selected cell = `(ctx.market, ctx.period)`.
- `BasisSelector` values: Month, YTD, MAT; MAT disabled for periods before 2025-05 with a tooltip "Needs 12 months of data". A URL with `basis=mat` for such a period shows Month and the title caption adds "MAT unavailable, showing Month".

## 3. Components
- `src/features/analyst/MarketGrid.tsx` (client): shadcn Table styled per §6.4; `role="grid"` is **not** used (it is a plain table); rows `tabindex=0`, Enter/Space select → `setContext({market})` (or `{product}` in product rows); selected row style; Status header has a `Tooltip` with `STATUS_RULE_TEXT` on an `info` icon button `aria-label="How status is determined"`; Spain's attainment cell gets the info icon with the tooltip text from preview-spec.md §9.2; `Sparkline` from `src/components/charts` (96 × 24, own min–max, `role="img"` label "24-month revenue trend, latest $1.68M"); AskWhy button per row.
- `src/components/charts/LineChart.tsx`: per design-system.md §6.10: fixed height 260, margins 48/16/16/32, y domain `[0, niceMax]` with 5 ticks, x = 24 band positions, three paths with the series tokens and dash arrays, grid lines, crosshair + tooltip (hover and keyboard: chart `tabindex=0`, ArrowLeft/Right move the active period, Home/End), anomaly layer as a second absolutely positioned SVG with `<g role="button" tabindex=0 aria-label="Anomaly: March 2026 revenue fell 27 percent versus February">` and the `−27% vs Feb` label in the main SVG; clicking or activating a period with Enter calls `onPeriod(period)` → `setContext({period})`. Table: 24 rows Period, Actual, Forecast, Prior year, Attainment, with the anomaly row flagged.
- `src/components/charts/RadarChart.tsx`: height 300; 3 axes at 120°; rings at 0.25 steps in `--grid`; series as closed paths with 2 px stroke in `--cat-n`, fill same colour at 12 % opacity; vertices as 4 px circles; legend always; each vertex focusable with label "{market}, {axis}: {raw value}"; table: one row per series with the three raw values.
- `src/components/charts/HeatMap.tsx`: `role="grid"` with `aria-rowcount/colcount`; row headers = market names (sticky), column headers = `Jun 24` style every 3rd label with all in `aria-label`; cells `role="gridcell" tabindex` roving, `aria-label="{market}, {Mon YYYY}, {x}% of forecast"`, background from scale with a 2 px `--surface` gap, selected cell gets a 2 px `--accent` inset outline; ArrowUp/Down/Left/Right, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown (±6 columns); Enter → `onCell(market, period)` → `setContext({market, period})`; tooltip on hover/focus with rows Actual, Forecast, Attainment; a legend of the 7 bins; table alt: markets × periods with the same numbers.
- `src/features/analyst/{TimeSeriesCard,RadarCard,HeatMapCard}.tsx`: card chrome, title, caption with provenance (`Derived · sum of series` for series charts; `Source · data.json` when the grid shows market KPIs), `ViewAsTable`.
- `src/features/analyst/Analyst.tsx` (server): basis selector row, grid (full width), then the time series full width, then Radar and HeatMap side by side from 1024 px (5/12 and 7/12), stacked below that; the Alert thresholds card follows the heat map. Rendered by `page.tsx` when `ctx.mode === "analyst"`. Layout in Analyst: KpiRow, then MapCard (the map stays in both modes), then Analyst (basis selector, grid, charts). TopMovers is hidden in Analyst.
