# 0008. Heat map as the fourth visualisation

Status: accepted, decided on 2026-11-02.

## Context

A fourth form is needed that supports pattern finding and coordinated views.

## Options

- Scatter of growth versus share.
- Heat map of markets by 24 periods, coloured by attainment.

## Decision

Heat map. A cell click sets market and period in the URL, driving the other views. It is a keyboard grid with a table alternative.

## Consequences

Germany's March 2026 dip reads at a glance. Scatter is listed under "with more time".
